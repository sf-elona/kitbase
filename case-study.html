Redesigning onboarding for Orbit Wallet

RoleLead Product Designer
Timeline10 weeks, 2025
Team1 PM, 2 engineers, 1 designer
Result34% fewer drop-offs
01Problem

Sign-up worked. Almost nobody finished it.

Orbit's sign-up flow collected everything a fintech app is legally required to ask for — in one 11-field form. Support tickets and session recordings agreed: most people gave up before reaching the last screen, and the ones who didn't often abandoned mid-verification days later.

The brief was narrow on purpose: don't redesign the whole app, fix the fifteen minutes between "download" and "first transfer."

02Process

Four weeks of research before a single screen

We split the existing flow into its real steps, watched where people actually stalled, then rebuilt around those moments instead of the form's own field order.

Week 1–2

Session review

60 recorded onboarding attempts, coded by drop-off point.

Week 3

Flow mapping

Redrew the flow as progressive steps instead of one long form.

Week 4–6

Prototyping

Three rounds of testable prototypes with 8 users each.

Week 7–10

Build & ship

Shipped behind a flag to 20% of new users, then everyone.

03Screens

One field per screen, verification moved earlier

The eleven fields became five short steps, each with a single clear task. Identity verification — previously the last, most-abandoned step — moved to the middle, right after the moment people had already committed emotionally by naming their first savings goal.

Step 1 — Goal
Step 2 — Verify
Step 3 — Fund
04Outcome

Fewer steps abandoned, faster to a first transfer

Rolled out gradually over three weeks, measured against the same cohort size as the original flow.

-34%

Onboarding drop-off, sign-up to first transfer

2.1×

+18pt
