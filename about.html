About

Made by one designer, in the open.

Hi — I'm Shafiatul Ferdous, a UI/UX designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I design digital products people love to use: turning complex workflows into simple, intuitive interfaces and building design systems that scale.

Kitbase is where I publish the reusable parts of that work. Every time I build a booking flow, a dashboard shell or a set of base components, I clean it up and give it away here — with the occasional premium pack to fund the time it takes.

Over the last couple of years I've designed across healthcare, fintech, SaaS, e-commerce and web platforms — running user interviews, shaping information architecture, and handing developers files they can actually build from.
